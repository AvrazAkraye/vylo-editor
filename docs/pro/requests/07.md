# Requests from 07 (Templates A)

Changes 07 needs in files it does not own. Nothing here was edited by 07.

## 1. Raise the plan prompt's budget (`app/test/motionai.test.mjs`) — needed for `npm test` to pass

`motionai.test.mjs` holds `planSystem()` to `BUDGET = 9500` characters. Every template's line goes into that prompt
(`motionai.ts`, `recipeLine`), and the prompt was already at 8,997 before the pro pass. With 07's eight templates it is
**10,510**, so the chain stops at that one assertion; every other step passes (checked by running all 159 steps one by one).
There is no way to fit eight templates into the 503 characters that were left: the id, `(overlay)`, the description and
each field's limit and hint take about 100 characters a template at the very least, and 07's take about 190.

Package 08 adds about as many templates and package 10 adds motion rules to the same prompt, so the integrator will need a
new number anyway. Suggested: `const BUDGET = 9500 + 200 * (Object.keys(META).length - 18)`, so the budget grows with
the catalogue and still catches a prompt that bloats per template; or a flat 13,000 after 08 and 10 land. 07's lines are
measured in `docs/pro/templates-a.md`.

## 2. Share the word-measuring helpers instead of a third copy

`motionrecipes-overlays.ts` keeps its measuring tables and helpers module-private (`LATIN`, `ARABIC_FORMS`, `arabicEm`,
`latinEm`, `lineWidth`, `widthOf`, `twoLines`, `roomy`, `Measure`) along with its looks (`inkOn`, `glassOf`, `SHEEN`,
`ACCENTS`, `marginOf`, `shines`). `motionrecipes-titles.ts` has a second, richer set (`setWords`, `balanced`, the faces).
07 could not import either, so `motionrecipes-pro-a.ts` carries a copy of the overlays set (about 250 lines, marked in its
header). Suggested: move them to one module (for example `motionrecipe-measure.ts`, exported, with its esbuild entry) that
the overlays, titles and both pro files import, then delete 07's copy. Pure move; no behaviour changes.

## 3. A shine should not decide the gallery's still (`motionanim.ts`, `stillTime`)

`stillTime` counts every layer's arrival, and a layer with no entrance "arrives" when it starts. A sweep of light that
starts after the words have landed therefore becomes the last arrival, and the still is drawn 0.35 s into it, with the
band a third of the way across the plate; in a gallery card it reads as a smudge. Measured: the core `lower-third` (still
1.85 s, band at 0.32 of its sweep) and `handle` (1.40 s, 0.35); `intro` lands exactly on its shine's first frame, where
nothing shows yet. 07 avoided it in its own templates (no shine on the plate lower thirds; the device's gleam is timed to finish by
the still; `test/pro-templates-a.test.mjs` checks it), but the core templates still show it. Suggested: in `stillTime`,
skip a layer whose only motion is a `shimmer` loop (no `in`, no `out`), as backdrops and particles are skipped.
