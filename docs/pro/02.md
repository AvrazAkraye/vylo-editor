# 02 Audio library: report

Branch `pro-02-audio`. The full account (API, every known answer, the WebKit measurements, how
to use it) is `docs/pro/audio.md`; credits are `docs/pro/credits/02.md`; things for the
integrator are `docs/pro/requests/02.md`.

## What exists

- `app/src/audiocore.ts`: dB and gain, clamp, mulberry32 and seeds, planar/interleaved,
  conform (equal lengths, NaN as silence), mixdown, mixInto, fades, soft clip, sample and true
  peak (4x Kaiser-windowed sinc, exact search that skips quiet stretches), biquads and their
  response, linear and windowed-sinc resampling (exact polyphase between 44.1 and 48 kHz), FFT
  and overlap-add convolution.
- `app/src/loudness.ts`: BS.1770-4 (K-weighting derived for any rate, 400 ms / 75% blocks,
  -70 LUFS and -10 LU gates), true peak, EBU 3342 loudness range, `loudnessPlan`,
  `normalizeLoudness`; the Phase 0 names kept (`Loudness`, `measureLoudness`, `gainToTarget`).
- `app/src/audioauto.ts`: lanes (`volume`, `rate`, `fx.<id>.<knob>`), a reader that clamps,
  `linear | hold | exp | bezier`, `valueAt`, `render`, `planLane` and `compile` onto an AudioParam.
- `app/src/audiofx.ts`: EQ, filter, compressor, limiter (look-ahead, optional true peak),
  delay, reverb (seeded room), each with a reader, a pure path (`renderChain`) and a builder
  (`buildFx`, `buildChain`); `chainEnvelopes` makes the dynamics preview exact; presets Warm,
  Clear, Room, Radio, Wide.
- `app/src/audioduck.ts`: speech finding and a ducking volume lane (x0.25, 0.15 s, 0.4 s).
- `app/test/pro-audio.test.mjs` (247 assertions, about 5 s) and `app/test/audiofake.mjs` (a
  recording fake AudioContext that plays the AudioParam timeline and runs a graph).

## Numbers

Loudness of 30 s stereo at 48 kHz: 45-60 ms (budget 150), 80-120 ms with nothing to skip.
Six-effect chain over 30 s stereo: 400-490 ms (budget 1.5 s). In the app's WebKit, the builders
equal `renderChain` bit for bit (EQ, filters, compressor and limiter with envelopes), within
2e-7 (reverb), 1.1e-4 (delay, a float32 delay time) and 1e-5 (a six-effect chain).

## Deviations

The delay's feedback is a finite series of echoes (to -80 dB), because WebKit and Chromium play a
feedback loop differently (measured: WebKit adds a 128-sample quantum per trip); dynamics are
exact in the preview only with `chainEnvelopes`; EQ and filters are IIRFilterNodes; effect-knob
automation is read but not rendered; the FFT carve is not done. Additions: loudness range,
`loudnessPlan`, `normalizeLoudness`, a true-peak limiter, the duck's lead and hold.

## Open

Not run in WebView2 (Windows). Not checked against a reference meter on real programme material.
No momentary or short-term maximum. Long, high-rate material untimed. Nothing for the integrator
to mount: 02 has no interface.
