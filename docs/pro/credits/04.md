# Credits: 04 Sound design

**No code was copied or translated from HyperFrames** (heygen-com/hyperframes, Apache-2.0) in this package, so no
file carries the Apache-2.0 header. HyperFrames' sound effects are recorded files (Pixabay), which RESEARCH.md says
not to take, and its audio graph is Web Audio and AudioWorklets; the effects here are synthesised from scratch in
plain JavaScript.

Numbers and ideas taken (they need no header, and are listed so the source is known):

- The delivery loudness, **−16 LUFS** with a **−1.5 dBTP** ceiling, and a static gain to reach it
  (`packages/studio-server/src/helpers/loudness.ts`, via `docs/pro/RESEARCH.md`).
- Music ducking's **0.15 s attack** and **0.4 s release** (RESEARCH.md, "Audio"). The depth is ours (×0.6, not ×0.25).

Vylo's own code reused, read-only: `videosynth.ts` (`arrange`, `render`, `MUSIC_MOODS`) composes and plays the music;
`motionanim.ts` (`countAt`, `gapOf`, `inDone`, `outStart`, `unitsOf`) times the cues; `motionmath.ts` (`rng`,
`hash01`, `easeOf`) seeds and shapes them.

Published techniques, written here from their descriptions: the trapezoidal ("TPT") state-variable filter
(V. Zavalishin, *The Art of VA Filter Design*); a look-ahead peak limiter (running minimum plus a moving average);
windowed-sinc interpolation for inter-sample peaks; the K-weighting and gating of ITU-R BS.1770-4, used only by the
test file and the measuring harness to report what an exact meter reads.
