# 01 Check: credits

**No code was copied, ported or closely translated from HyperFrames** (heygen-com/hyperframes, Apache-2.0), so no
source file carries an Apache-2.0 header. `motioncheck.ts` and `MotionChecks.tsx` are written for Motion's document
model: HyperFrames measures a rendered page in a browser; Motion's check is arithmetic over the document.

Ideas and numbers taken from it, for the record (read in the local clone, nothing run):

| From | What |
|---|---|
| `packages/cli/src/commands/layout-audit.browser.js` | text overlap above 20% of the smaller block; text occlusion at 15% for prose; a panel past the frame by more than max(24 px, 2.5% of the short edge) — Motion's 2.5u bleed; counting coverage by probe points over each text rectangle (Motion samples a grid per line of words) |
| `packages/cli/src/commands/contrast-audit.browser.js` | WCAG 4.5, or 3.0 for text of 24 px or 19 px at weight 700 and above; compositing a translucent text colour over its ground before measuring |
| `packages/cli/src/utils/checkPipeline.ts` | the shape of a check: findings with a rule, a severity and a moment; 3 s with nothing changing |
| `packages/studio/src/utils/previewSafeMargins.ts` | title-safe 80% and action-safe 90%, the starting point for the safe margin (tuned to 5u) |
| `docs/pro/RESEARCH.md` (from the skills) | reading speed of about 2.5 words a second, captions on screen at least 0.5 s, a hero visible by 0.5 s |

One difference between RESEARCH.md and the code, as RESEARCH.md asks to be told: the digest says `text_occluded` is
"15% or more covered"; the code flags an atomic label at any hit and applies 15% to prose only
(`DEFAULT_PROSE_COVERAGE_FLOOR`). Motion uses 15% for both, because a label under a solid shape at under 15% is most
often a designed overlap (a badge edge), and false alarms are worse than misses.
