## Ideas and numbers (no code copied)

- `packages/core/src/audio/audioFxGraph.ts`, `synthesizeReverbImpulse`: a reverb as a **seeded
  synthetic impulse** of decaying, low-passed noise, scaled to **unit energy** so preview and
  render convolve one room. `reverbImpulse` here is its own generator (mulberry32, an RT60
  exponential decay, a damping filter that closes along the tail, decorrelated stereo).
- `packages/core/src/audioFx.ts`: an effect as a descriptor of knobs in the units a person thinks
  in, read through a function that clamps every knob and treats null and "" as missing, not 0.
- `packages/studio-server/src/helpers/loudness.ts`: delivery at **-16 LUFS** under a
  **-1.5 dBTP** ceiling, one static gain, and a plan that names what limited it
  (`loudnessPlan.limitedBy`). Their meter is FFmpeg's; this one is written here.
- `packages/core/src/audioCarve.ts`: speech is what is louder than **30 dB under the voice's own
  peak** (`audioduck.ts`'s `range`).
- RESEARCH.md's numbers from HyperFrames: music ducks to **x0.25** with a **0.15 s attack** and a
  **0.4 s release**.

## Other sources (not HyperFrames)

- ITU-R BS.1770-4 (K-weighting, gating, true peak) and EBU Tech 3341 / 3342 (the test signals and
  the loudness range). The analogue K-weighting prototype values (f0, gain and Q of the shelf and
  the high-pass) that reproduce the standard's 48 kHz coefficients at any rate are the ones
  libebur128 (MIT) uses and `videosynth.ts` already used: numbers, not code.
- The shelf and peak formulas are the Web Audio specification's (the Audio EQ Cookbook, R.
  Bristow-Johnson); the compressor's soft-knee curve is from Giannoulis, Massberg and Reiss,
  "Digital Dynamic Range Compressor Design: A Tutorial and Analysis", JAES 2012.
- mulberry32 (Tommy Ettinger, public domain).
