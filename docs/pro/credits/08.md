# Credits: 08 Templates B and visuals

No code was copied or translated from HyperFrames (heygen-com/hyperframes, Apache-2.0); every line in
`motionbackdrop.ts`, `motioncharts.ts` and `motionrecipes-pro-b*.ts` is this repository's own, written for Canvas2D
without GSAP, shaders or `ctx.filter`. So no file carries an Apache-2.0 header. These looks were studied (read only)
and their ideas taken, which the licence does not require crediting but which is recorded here:

- `registry/components/grain-overlay`: one noise texture moved to a new offset many times a second, stepped, reads as
  film grain. Here: three noise tiles made once, a new tile, offset, turn and mirroring every film frame.
- `registry/components/vignette`: a radial darkening from a clear centre. Here: an ellipse between the frame's shape and
  a circle, with a breath.
- `registry/blocks/organic-light-leak-overlay`: warm glows coming in at the sides with a hot core and a diagonal gold
  streak, screen-blended, blooming and fading. Here: one to three leaks, a hot core, an optional streak and a warm wash,
  each blooming once a cycle so the loop closes.
- `registry/blocks/halftone-field`: a grid of dots whose size follows a flowing field. Here: a dot screen at a press
  angle over a field of travelling waves and a ring (no noise), dot area following the field.
- `registry/blocks/bar-chart-race`: ranked bars overtaking as periods advance, the axis rescaling with ticks above the
  plot, the accent handed to whoever leads, the period large at the end of the heading row, and the field format
  `Name: v1, v2, …` with comma-separated periods. Here: a chart kind with its own data model, monotone interpolation,
  time-averaged places and cross-fading ticks.

No media, fonts, icons or sounds were taken.
