# Credits: 07 Templates A

**No code was copied or translated from HyperFrames** (heygen-com/hyperframes, Apache-2.0). Every item below is HTML
animated by a GSAP timeline, and GSAP's licence is not open source, so the rule in `docs/pro/RESEARCH.md` applies: the
looks may be learned from, the code may not be taken. Nothing in `app/src/motionrecipes-pro-a.ts` or
`app/src/motionrecipes-pro-a-meta.ts` therefore carries an Apache-2.0 header. What was taken is the idea of a kind of
graphic, read from the item's manifest (and, for `lt-mask-reveal`, its CSS); each was then designed again in Motion's own
vocabulary (pins and u, `grow`/`wipe`/`mask`/`draw`, the palette tokens) and no real product's look was imitated.
