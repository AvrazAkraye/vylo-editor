# 05 Scenes and transitions: credits

**No code was copied or translated from HyperFrames** (heygen-com/hyperframes, Apache-2.0). Its transitions are GSAP
timelines over DOM elements and WebGL shaders; Motion's are Canvas2D composites written for this repository, so
no file carries the Apache-2.0 header. What was taken is ideas and numbers, listed here so the debt is visible.

| From (in the HyperFrames clone) | Taken | Where it is used |
|---|---|---|
| `skills/hyperframes-animation/transitions/overview.md`, "Animation Rules for Multi-Scene Compositions" | The rule that exits are not played before a transition: *the transition is the exit*; only the last scene exits | `motionscene.ts`: a scene holds still at its hold moment until the cut when the next scene arrives with a transition |
| same file, "Energy → Primary Transition" | Pick one primary transition for most cuts | `primaryTransition`: a new scene arrives with the graphic's most used transition |
| same file, "Presets" and "Energy" tables | Lengths and curves: calm moves 0.5 to 0.8 s on a sine curve, medium 0.3 to 0.5 s on power2/power3, high-energy 0.15 to 0.3 s | `TRANSITION_LOOKS` in `motiontransition.ts` (fade 0.5 s soft, push 0.5 s inout, glitch 0.3 s, light leak 0.8 s soft, …) and `LIMITS.transitionMin` 0.15 s |
| `.../transitions/css-push.md` (Push Slide, Vertical Push) | Both scenes move together, the new pushing the old out, 0.5 s power3.inOut | `push`, `whip` |
| `.../transitions/css-radial.md` (Circle Iris) | An expanding circle from the centre reveals the new scene | `iris` |
| `.../transitions/css-scale.md` (Zoom Through) | The old scene zooms past the camera and fades while the new one settles | `zoom` (the numbers differ: here the new scene settles from 1.2 so it always covers the frame) |
| `.../transitions/css-cover.md` (Horizontal Blinds) | Strips across the frame | `blinds` |
| `.../transitions/css-light.md` (Light Leak, Overexposure Burn) | A warm tint plus bright radial blobs washing across; a flash to white at the swap | `light-leak`, `flash` |
| `docs/pro/RESEARCH.md` (this repository's digest of HyperFrames) | Which shader transitions are doable in Canvas2D without `ctx.filter`, and that the glitch's colour split can only be approximated (offset copies with multiply, then lighter) | the list of kinds, and `glitch` |

The HyperFrames licence is Apache-2.0 (`LICENSE` at the root of the clone). Ideas and numbers need no header under
`docs/PRO.md`'s rule; if a reviewer judges any of the above closer than that, the header to add is
*"Portions derived from HyperFrames (heygen-com/hyperframes, Apache-2.0), `<path>`. Changed: rewritten for Canvas2D."*
