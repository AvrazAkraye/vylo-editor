# Package `ui`: the Broadcast screens

Read `docs/WA.md` first, then the briefs of `audience`, `engine`, `templates-a` and `writer` — **you build against their contracts** (the placeholders in `src/` already have the final names and signatures, so it compiles today; the real modules arrive when the integrator merges). You write the screens; you do not write parsing, sending, templates or prompts.

## Why
The owner wants to send bulk messages on WhatsApp, load numbers from a file, pick or have the AI write a promotional message, and be sure it works — in a module that looks nicer and more professional. This is the part they will see and touch. It must be so clear that a shopkeeper who has never sent a broadcast does it correctly the first time, and so honest that they understand the risks before they press Send.

## You own
`app/src/WhatsAppBroadcast.tsx` (replace the placeholder; keep `BroadcastProps`, add to it only optional props), `app/src/WhatsAppAudience.tsx`, `app/src/WhatsAppCompose.tsx`, `app/src/WhatsAppTemplates.tsx`, `app/src/WhatsAppRun.tsx`, the CSS between `/* wa:bulk start */` and `/* wa:bulk end */` (logical properties only), your i18n entries (`// wa ui`), `app/test/wa-ui.test.mjs`, `app/test/wa-ui-more.test.mjs`, `docs/wa/ui.md`, `docs/wa/review-needed.md` (your section). The panel `WhatsAppPanel.tsx` belongs to `design`: the door (`Broadcast` button, the `bulk` state, mounting `<WhatsAppBroadcast …/>`) is already there.

## The flow (three steps, then a run) — one obvious next button, one plain sentence for whatever is wrong
**Header**: back/close, "Broadcast", the account it sends from (name + number from `Account`), and a small "History" button.
1. **People** — four ways in, as tabs: *Paste* (a big box: "Paste numbers, or a whole list with names"), *From a file* (drop zone + Browse; `.txt .csv .tsv .vcf .xlsx`; the app's own file-reading path — see how `WhatsAppPanel.tsx` attaches a file, `pickFile` and `invoke`), *From my chats* (`fromChats(chats)` of the account's chats; you are given none yet — accept an optional prop `chats?: readonly Chat[]`), *Saved lists* (`loadAudiences`).
   After a read: a summary that is also the main control — "**1,204 people** · 37 couldn't be read · 12 repeated · 9 asked not to be messaged" (the last from `loadSuppressed` + `excludeSuppressed`); the rejected lines in a collapsed list with the reason in plain words; a small table of the first rows with **column choices** ("Phone is in: [select]", "Name is in: [select]" — re-run `parseAudience` with the overrides) and a **country** select (the code assumed for numbers written without one; default `countryForLang(lang)`, remembered). *Save this list* with a name. A tiny honest line: "Only your own contacts and customers who agreed to hear from you."
2. **Message** — a text area (auto-direction, `dir="auto"`), **variable chips** (`{name}`, `{first_name}`, one per file column) that insert at the cursor, a **Ready messages** button opening the library (`WhatsAppTemplates.tsx`: category chips, search box, cards with a language-aware preview and a *Use this* button; `fillTemplate` with a small form for the business name and the placeholders the person must fill: show which `{…}` are still empty), **Write with AI** (a brief box, tone select, count 1–4; results as cards with *Use this*, *Improve*, *Shorten*, *Translate to…*; `writeMessages`; needs `gw`+`efforts` — without a route say plainly "Add a model in Settings" and offer `onProviders`), an **attachment** button (image, video, document, voice note, contact card — a small form; size limit and unreadable files explained), the **opt-out** toggle with the line shown and editable (`OPT_OUT[lang]`), the message **language** (en/ar/ckb/kmr; defaults to the interface's), a **live preview** as a WhatsApp-like bubble for three real people from the list (`renderMessage`), a character count, `missingVars` warnings ("12 people have no city — their message will skip it"), and `riskHints` as gentle one-line tips (never blocking).
3. **Review & send** — one card: *To* (the number of people; sample masked numbers), *From* (the account), *Message* (the exact rendered text of the first person; attachment name), *Pace* (default shown plainly: "about one message every 12–30 s, a break every 20 · about 3 h 10 min · 200 a day at most"; *Change pace* reveals the controls inside `PACE_BOUNDS`, with the plain warning above `CAP_WARN` and the days it will take when the list is longer than the cap), **the consent tick** ("Everyone on this list agreed to hear from me." — required), **Send a test to myself** (asks for the person's own number once and remembers it; `sendTest`), the list of `validateCampaign` problems in words, and the **Send** button, disabled until there are no problems; for more than 50 people it asks the person to **type the number of people** to confirm. Above the button, in small type: "WhatsApp can block numbers that message people who did not ask. Messages go slowly on purpose."
4. **Run** (`WhatsAppRun.tsx`) — big progress (ring or bar), counts (sent, failed, skipped, unknown), the person being messaged now (masked), "next message in 18 s" / "break until 14:20" / "daily limit reached — continues tomorrow" from `RunEvent`s, **Pause / Resume / Stop**, a live list of the last outcomes, and when a runner halts, the reason in a plain sentence with what to do (`auth` → "The WhatsApp server refused the key. Check the connection in settings."; `not-connected`; `rate-limited`; `repeated-failures`; `account`; `storage`). Closing the panel does not stop a run; reopening shows it (the runner is held at module level; `recover` after a restart shows "paused — it was interrupted" and *Continue*).
5. **Report** — totals, a table with a standing per person (filterable: failed, unknown, skipped), **Download CSV** (`reportCsv`; save the way the app saves other files — find `export_write_*`/`save` patterns), *Messages that may or may not have been sent* explained, and *Add stop replies to the do-not-contact list* (runs `optOutPhones` over the chats' messages you are given via an optional prop) plus a *Do-not-contact list* manager (view, remove).
**History**: past campaigns (name, date, state, counts) with Open report, Duplicate as a new draft, Delete.
**Assistant-prepared drafts** (`Campaign.staged`): shown first, on top: "Prepared by the assistant — nothing has been sent" and open straight at step 3 with the message and people already there.

## Rules of this screen
- **Nothing sends without the person pressing Send on step 3**, and Send is the only place `runCampaign` is called. Test it.
- Keep the work if the panel is closed: the draft (text, template, pace, country, language) in `localStorage` (`vylo.whatsapp.bulk.draft.v1`, read through a reader that clamps), the people in IndexedDB via `saveAudience`. *Start over* clears.
- Responsive: the 248 px column (one step at a time, a sticky bottom bar with Back/Next, no horizontal scroll) and full screen (steps on the left, the live preview and summary on the right). Four languages and RTL; keyboard from start to finish (tab order, Enter/Space, Escape closes a drawer and returns focus); `aria-live` for progress; sufficient contrast in both themes; 44 px targets in the column.
- Every word through `t()`; sentences are whole (no concatenated fragments); numbers with `fill`.
- Never show a key or the gateway address; masks for numbers except on the report.
- Look and feel: read `WhatsAppPanel.tsx`, `MotionPanel.tsx`/`MotionExport.tsx`/`MotionHome.tsx` and their CSS and `design`'s brief; the same tokens, radii, spacing and icon set; calm, spacious, one accent colour; WhatsApp-like green only in the message preview. No new dependency, no images other than inline SVG/emoji.

## How to build and prove it with no engine yet
Build against the placeholders and write fakes inside your tests (a fake `parseAudience`, a fake runner emitting `RunEvent`s). Render with `react-dom/server` the way `test/motionstage.test.mjs` does and drive pure helpers directly. For looks, use the headless Chrome-over-CDP harness described in the project memory (`vylo-panel-visual-check`: a harness folder in your git-ignored `app/.test-build/`, the whole app or the panel rendered with fake props at 320 px and 1100 px, light and dark, `en`/`ar`/`ckb`, LTR/RTL); keep the Chrome profile on the SSD and delete it afterwards; macOS has no `timeout` (`perl -e 'alarm 60; exec @ARGV'`). Put contact sheets in `/Volumes/ExtremeSSD/apps/vylo-wa-samples/ui/`.

## Not yours
Parsing, number rules, the sending loop, storage, template content, prompts, the panel's chat views, the assistant's tools.
